# Acceptance M3 – Completion

Package ACC-M3, 2026-11-23. Checks WP-M3-01 (full E2E matrix), WP-M3-08 (draft PRs), the traceability rule and the
acceptance criteria of `docs/platform/spec/09-milestones.md` (M3), takes over the results of the other M3 packages
and accepts the smartphone track M2f (section "M2f" in `M2.md`). The same file exists in `agent-core` and `agent`.

PostgreSQL and browser; the **second device** hosts hub, PostgreSQL and browser in T2; the **workstation** is the
machine the packages are developed on. Decisions: `docs/platform/decisions/ACC-M3.md`.

**Result: M3 accepted with two deferred exceptions.** The full E2E matrix is green three times per topology and
project (one lane per combination also on the end state including PFM-01 and KOR-13), every requirement has a
passing test (traceability 153 of 153), all coverage gates are 100 %, the scans are clean, the draft PRs are current
and R-P.11 holds. Not met at this acceptance and handed to ACC-FINAL: the two **open high-severity defects** of
PFM-01 (PERF-03 on T2, PERF-03 under PERF-05; correction KOR-13) and, with them, the PERF acceptance (WP-M3-03);
the manual PRF rows need a person.
