# KOR-62 – contract changes

ADR-027 section 3 (additive). Account of an AI agent alone (rule E-27, ADR-071, `11-agent-access.md` §5.5).

## `api/agent-accounts-create.request.json`, `api/agent-accounts-create.response.json` (new)

`POST /v1/agent-accounts` (anon, `INDEX.json` owner KOR-62, spec list `tests/platform/contracts/spec/endpoints-kor62.json`):
identity, recovery slot and `cred_wrap` as for the first passkey of `register/finish`, the agent with its sealed
identity, label and claim hash, and the named proof (`proof.method`, today only `agent-signature-v1`, with `inv_sig`,
`id_sig`, `agent_sig`). Answer `{user_id, agent_id}`. Fixtures valid/invalid.

## `api/recovery-finish.request.json`, `api/recovery-finish.response.json` (extended)

Optional `agent` (the new agent, `method`, `agent_sig`); the response is now one of the passkey registration options
`{ceremony_id, options}` (unchanged) or `{user_id, agent_id}` for a recovery with an agent. Old requests and answers
stay valid. Fixtures `valid/agent.json`, `invalid/agent-without-signature.json`, `invalid/agent-and-ceremony.json`.

## `audit/agent.register.json`, `enums/audit-actions.json` (new action)

`agent.register` (group `agent`): an agent became the main orchestrator without a pairing – a registration
(`invite_id`) or a recovery without a passkey; `agent` and `method` required. Fixtures valid/invalid.

## `vectors/agent.json` (new section)

`own_account`: the account and recover texts, their signatures (invitation key, identity, agent key) and the proof
methods; negative vectors of the operations `verify_account` and `verify_recover`. Regenerated by
`contracts/tools/make_vectors.py`; checked by the hub, the runner copy and the web client.
