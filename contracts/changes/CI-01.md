# CI-01 – self-hosted CI jobs, stage 1

Spec: `docs/platform/spec/16-ci-runners.md` section 17 (decisions D-12 and D-13 of section 13).

- `outbox/objects.json#/$defs/body_runtime`: `vm_kind` gains `ci` – the runtime of a CI VM (one GitHub Actions job),
  attributed like a work VM to one session: the session that created the job's pull request, or the main session of
  the repository's fixed task. The hub prices it by its size and books it to that session's task and workgroup.
  Fixture `fixtures/outbox/runtime/valid/ci-vm.json`.
- New runner audit action `ci.job` (`enums/audit-actions.json`, group `runner`; `enums/outbox-audit-actions.json`;
  `audit/ci.job.json`, inlined into `outbox/objects.json#/$defs/body_audit`): one step of a job – `accepted`,
  `refused` (written under the repository's fixed task) or `ended` – with the event, the basis of its task, the
  reason, GitHub's result, the size, the duration and the work VM. Only IDs and closed codes: no repository, branch,
  workflow or person names. Fixtures `fixtures/audit/ci.job/` and `fixtures/outbox/audit/valid/ci-job.json`.
